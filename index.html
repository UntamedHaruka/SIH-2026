THE PROBLEM

Safety training shouldn't
wait for an accident.

Traditional training often stops at manuals, lectures and checklists. SafeForge turns safety procedures into interactive experiences where trainees can practice decisions without real-world risk.

The platform combines a Unity-based industrial environment, scenario-driven assessments, performance analytics and a lightweight AR prototype for real-world equipment guidance.
